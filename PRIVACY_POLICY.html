Summary

Royal Ludo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you. The app has no account system, no sign-in, no chat, no location access, and does not save any data to servers we control. All game progress exists only in memory for the duration of a match on your device and is not saved or uploaded anywhere.

The only data handling in this app comes from the third-party advertising SDK it uses to show ads, described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Royal Ludo is free to play and supported by ads served through Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads). To show ads (a banner on the main menu and an interstitial ad between matches), the AdMob SDK may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • App interaction data relevant to ad delivery and measurement (e.g. ad impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer of this app, according to Google's own privacy policy and AdMob's data usage terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (e.g. Android Settings > Privacy > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalization"), or through Google's Ad Settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand basic usage patterns - which screens are viewed and a small number of gameplay events (starting a match with a given number of human/bot players, who won a match). This does not include your name, email, or any other personally-identifying information. Firebase Analytics may use device and advertising identifiers for this purpose, processed by Google per:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

No other data collection

This app does not:

  • Require or support account creation or login.
  • Collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, precise location, or files.
  • Sell any data - the app collects none to sell.

No real-money content

Royal Ludo is a free, casual board game. It does not involve gambling, wagering, or real-money prizes of any kind, and does not process payments.

Children's privacy

Royal Ludo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If ads are configured for a general audience, standard AdMob content guidelines apply; the developer is responsible for configuring Play Console's "Ads" and "Target audience" declarations correctly (e.g. enabling child-directed treatment / tagging if the app is ever positioned for a young audience) and for reviewing Google's Families Policy requirements if targeting children specifically: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (e.g. a new SDK is added), this policy will be updated and the "Effective date" above will reflect the most recent revision.
